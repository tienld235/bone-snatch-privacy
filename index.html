Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Bone Snatch is an offline party game made by TieChan Software. Players share one phone and take turns; you do not create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts or location.

Data stored on your device

Your coins, the dogs, bones and scenes you unlocked, the number of players and bones you last chose, and your language, sound and vibration settings are saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. If you have turned on Android Backup, Android may copy this data into your own Google account so it comes back when you reinstall; that copy belongs to your account and is never sent to us.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address and technical information about your device. This is handled by Google, not by us, and is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

How data is kept safe

We run no servers and receive no personal data. Game data stays in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Data used for ads is transmitted by Google's SDK over encrypted connections.

Keeping and deleting data

Game data is kept until you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android Settings → Apps → Bone Snatch → Storage. To also remove a backup copy, delete it from your Google account's backups. Data collected for advertising is kept and deleted according to Google's policy above.

Children

This app is rated for players 13 and older and is not directed at children.

Phần này do Google xử lý, không phải chúng tôi, và được gửi qua kết nối mã hoá (HTTPS). Bạn có thể đặt lại hoặc xoá mã quảng cáo trong Cài đặt Android → Quyền riêng tư → Quảng cáo.
